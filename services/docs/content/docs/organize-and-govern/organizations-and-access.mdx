---
title: Organizations and access
description: Organize security work, invite collaborators, and control shared access.
---

Organizations provide shared workspaces for scans, registries, policies, tokens, notifications, and audit history. Invite members from the organization page and assign the least privileged role that lets them complete their work.

Organization tokens are designed for automation. Give CI tokens only the required scopes, store them in the CI secret manager, and revoke a token immediately when it is exposed or no longer needed.

Personal resources can be transferred to another owner or shared with an organization when teams need continuity without recreating scan history.
