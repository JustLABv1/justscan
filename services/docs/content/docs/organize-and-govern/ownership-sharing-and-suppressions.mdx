---
title: Ownership, sharing, and suppressions
description: Share evidence deliberately and record accepted-risk decisions.
---

JustScan tracks ownership for scans, registries, suppressions, and status pages. Owners can transfer a resource or grant organization access without making it globally visible.

Suppressions hide accepted findings from the relevant view. Keep each suppression narrow, explain why it is safe, and remove it when a fixed image is available. Use comments and manual findings to preserve team context alongside the scanner output.
